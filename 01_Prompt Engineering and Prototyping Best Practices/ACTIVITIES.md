# Discussion Question

1. What are some limitations of vibe checking as an evaluation tool?

Vibe checking is inherently subjective, and non-replicable from person-to-person. It is difficult to know how the system will response to someone else with questions phrased slightly differently. There are only so many iterations I can do on my own, and they are inherently subject to personal cognitive biases. There is also no "truth" or verifiable benchmark that can be repeatedly tested. With language, style and tone can be ascertained from text chat conversations, the overall objective factual correctness cannot. This is also a highly manual exercise, and will not scale with large corpuses and/or sets of agents.
